How we work

Clarity is part of the result.

Our purpose is to improve understanding of advanced AI through careful evaluation, verification, and open research. We do not build or train new foundation models. We aim to make research inspectable and useful to the wider community.

We distinguish measured results from interpretation, and completed work from ongoing work. When evidence is limited, the claim stays limited too.

❓Explicit questionsState what the research is intended to resolve.
📝Recorded methodsDescribe conditions and procedures clearly.
📁Preserved evidenceKeep results traceable to their supporting materials.
🧭Honest limitationsMake uncertainty and unresolved work visible.
Open collaboration

🤝Help us ask better questions.

Researchers, engineers, and organizations can review the public work, identify gaps, and propose collaborations through the repository. We welcome substantive criticism and reproducibility efforts.
