Evidence-led perspective

Analysis without hype.

We aim to help decision-makers understand the forces shaping machine learning and robotics. We will separate documented facts from interpretation, identify whose perspective a claim represents, and state when evidence is incomplete.

Our goal is understanding, not promotion. We do not present a forecast as a fact or an industry perspective as a research finding.

Traceable sourcesLink claims to public evidence wherever possible.
Clear statusLabel published analysis, work in progress, and open questions.
Visible uncertaintyState assumptions, limitations, and competing explanations.
Open participationInvite informed criticism and constructive collaboration.
Contribute to the discussion

🤝Bring evidence. Challenge assumptions.
